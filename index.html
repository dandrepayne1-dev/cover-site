The money shows up before the system does.

NIL deals, endorsement checks, and 1099 income arrive fast. No withholding. No system. No clear picture of where the money is going. By April, the tax bill is real and the money is not.

CØVER™ is the system. We organize the numbers, show you exactly where you stand, build the next steps, and keep you accountable to the plan. One team that knows the whole picture, instead of four people who each know a piece.

The Ladder // Every Athlete Starts With The Baseline

Three steps. In order.

Ø1

Baseline Assessment

A full read on where your money actually stands: income sources, tax exposure, and spending pattern, turned into a clear starting point.

Ø2

Monthly Financial Report

Your financial activity is reviewed every month and turned into a concise written report: exact numbers, named priorities, and specific actions with dates on them.

Ø3

Quarterly Review

A deeper look every 90 days: progress against the plan, tax readiness ahead of deadlines, savings targets, and what changes before next season.

Your assessment tells us where you start.

The Baseline Assessment assigns your tier: Foundation, Growth, or Elite. The tier sets which stage of the Long Game you enter and how deep the work goes. Nobody is behind, and nobody starts over.
